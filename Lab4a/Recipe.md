# Tomato Soup
## Ingredients
  - Many ripe tomatoes
  - One Red Onion
  - Two large clumps of garlic
  - Vegetable stock
  - Heavy cream
  - Olive oil
  ### Seasoning
  - Cayenne Pepper
  - Salt
  - Pepper
  - Cumin
  - Basil Leaves
## Steps
1. Prepare a baking tray and set the oven to 400°C
2. Wash all produce
3. Slice tomatoes in half and lay on baking sheet, skin up
4. Slice red onion into four even size pieces and lay on baking sheet
5. slice the tip off the garlic bunches and lay them facing up on the baking sheet
6. Pour olive oil, and season with salt and pepper over top of everything
7. Place tray into oven for 25 minutes
8. Remove tray from oven and set up blender
9. Place all tomatoes, the onion, and squeeze the garlic into the blender
10. add some vegetable oil, cayenne pepper, basil leaves and heavy cream into blender
11. blend
12. Serve in a bowl alongside a grilled cheese sandwich
13. Done
